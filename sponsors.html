Why Sponsor BIB

The Largest Student-Run Event in Pittsburgh

Bhangra in the Burgh is an entirely student-run Bhangra competition. Since our inception in 2007, BIB has been a platform for top Bhangra teams from across the nation to showcase their talent. This colorful exhibition of the positive energy of Bhangra helps us raise funds for a charity annually. Our sold-out shows and immense growth have raised over $110,000 for charitable causes in the community. Bhangra in the Burgh brings together students from Pittsburgh and across the nation, making it the largest student-run event in Pittsburgh.
