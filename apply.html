Required Reading

Code of Conduct

Bhangra in the Burgh maintains a Sexual Misconduct Policy with a zero-tolerance approach to sexual violence in any form. All competing teams, exhibition teams, roster members, and BIB staff are required to read and sign this policy.

Teams must sign during the registration period in order to submit an application. Once accepted, every team member must sign as well in order to compete.

To report an incident or notify our Risk Team, email bhangraintheburgh@gmail.com.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

When will registration open?
Registration for BIB XX will be announced soon. Follow @bhangraintheburgh for the announcement, and check back here for the link to our registration portal.
How do I apply?
There are two ways to apply to BIB XX. The first is our application portal, which will be linked on this page — you can enter all your information there, including basic information, audition video, registration fee, and roster. The second is our application packet, which will be available on our website and through social media. The PDF version of the packet should be emailed to bhangraintheburgh@gmail.com.
Who can apply?
We welcome all collegiate teams to apply. If you have any specific concerns about your team's eligibility, please email us at bhangraintheburgh@gmail.com.
How many teams get accepted?
Bhangra in the Burgh admits 8 to 9 Bhangra teams every year.
How can I attend the show?
We will be releasing information regarding tickets in the fall, so stay tuned. We will also have a live stream for those who cannot see the show in person.
